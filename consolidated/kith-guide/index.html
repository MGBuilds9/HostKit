The Kith

Building amenities

Residents enjoy over 12,000 sq ft of amenity space across the building.

  • Fitness centre
  • Basketball court with running track
  • Theatre room
  • Co-working lounge with Wi-Fi
  • Party room
  • Outdoor terrace with BBQs
  • DIY workshop
  • Pet wash station

Garbage and recycling

There is a garbage chute on the 14th floor near your unit. It takes recycling and organics. Larger items go to the garbage room with the blue bins on the parking level.

Erin Mills

Around the building

Everything below is a short walk or drive from The Kith.

  • Erin Mills Town Centre. Shopping and dining, about a two minute walk across the street.
  • Walmart, 5100 Erin Mills Pkwy. Grocery and pharmacy, open 8 AM to 10 PM daily. Steps away.
  • Credit Valley Hospital. About a two minute walk, just across Eglinton Avenue West.
  • Transit. Bus stops right across the street, plus Erin Mills Station nearby with MiWay, GO Bus, and Oakville Transit connections. Quick access to Highways 403 and 407.
