# Airbnb Automated Messaging Playbook
*HostKit Standard Operating Procedures — Unit 1423 (and Unit 1523)*

This playbook contains the exact message templates to configure in **Airbnb Scheduled Messages** or send directly via HostKit. These messages are designed to eliminate arrival anxiety, provide flawless self-check-in guidance, and ensure building compliance with PSCC 1190.

---

## 1. Instant Booking Confirmation
* **Trigger**: Sent automatically immediately upon booking confirmation.
* **Purpose**: Confirm details, set expectations, provide early guide link.

```text
Hi {{guest_first_name}},

Thank you for choosing our apartment at The Kith Condominiums in Mississauga! We are thrilled to host you.

Here are your reservation details:
• Check-In: {{check_in_date}} after 3:00 PM
• Check-Out: {{check_out_date}} by 11:00 AM
• Address: 2485 Eglinton Avenue West, Unit 1423, Mississauga, ON L5M 2V8

To help you prepare, here is your digital guest guide with photos, Wi-Fi info, and local tips:
https://hostkit.mkgbuilds.com/g/kith-1423

A quick note on building etiquette:
Our condo is a quiet, private residential community. We maintain strict non-smoking and quiet hours (10:00 PM – 8:00 AM). Check-in is 100% self-guided, with keys waiting inside your unit.

Will you be driving and needing our reserved underground parking stall (Spot P3-257)? Let us know so we can prepare your parking access.

Looking forward to hosting you!
Michael
```

## 2. 48 Hours Prior to Arrival
* **Trigger**: 2 days before check-in at 10:00 AM.
* **Purpose**: Confirm arrival timing and transportation mode.

```text
Hi {{guest_first_name}},

We're getting Unit 1423 prepped for your arrival this {{check_in_day}}!

Could you please share your estimated arrival time? Also, please let us know if you'll be arriving by car or by transit/rideshare so we can ensure the right gate access is ready for you.

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. We'll send your access instructions the morning of your arrival.

Safe travels,
Michael
```

## 3. Check-In Day — Morning Brief (10:00 AM)
* **Trigger**: Check-in day at 10:00 AM.
* **Purpose**: Intercom instructions, parking flow, avoiding concierge friction.

```text
Good morning {{guest_first_name}},

Unit 1423 will be ready for you at 3:00 PM today!

Here are the key details for your arrival:
📍 Address: 2485 Eglinton Avenue West, Mississauga, ON
🚪 Intercom / Buzzer: Scroll to "George A." on the directory to buzz in.
🔑 Keys & Fob: Resting on the kitchen island counter inside the unit.

🚗 If Arriving by Car:
1. Enter the garage and park temporarily in Visitor Parking on Level P1/P2.
2. Take the elevator to the 14th floor, enter Unit 1423, and pick up the garage fob and keys from the kitchen counter.
3. Return to your car, tap the fob at the yellow gate to enter resident parking, and park in Stall P3-257.

🚶 If Arriving by Uber / Transit:
Simply buzz "George A." at the front entrance, take the elevator straight to the 14th floor, and collect your keys inside.

⚠️ Private Residence Note:
Concierge and security do not manage guest check-ins or key handovers. You do not need to speak with the desk; your keys are waiting inside for you. If you need anything at all, text or call me directly at (647) 470-9434.

Interactive arrival guide: https://hostkit.mkgbuilds.com/g/kith-1423

See you soon!
Michael
```

## 6. Day Before Departure (6:00 PM)
* **Trigger**: Day before checkout at 6:00 PM.
* **Purpose**: Stress-free departure instructions and 11:00 AM deadline.

```text
Hi {{guest_first_name}},

We hope you've had a wonderful stay in Mississauga!

Just a reminder that checkout tomorrow ({{check_out_date}}) is by 11:00 AM to allow our cleaning team time to prepare for the next guests.

Our checkout checklist is simple:
1. Place used bathroom towels in the bathtub (no need to strip beds).
2. Put any dirty dishes in the dishwasher and start the cycle.
3. Drop personal garbage bags down the 14th floor chute down the hall.
4. Leave the keys and garage fob on the kitchen counter and ensure the door clicks securely locked behind you.

Safe travels on your journey home!
Michael
```

```text
Warmly,
Michael
```
